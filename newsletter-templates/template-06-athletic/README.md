# Template 06 — Athletic

Bold, sporty training-log direction (620px). Heavy uppercase/italic headings, near-black blocks with a lime accent next to brand green, a colour stripe, a "session of the week" timer block, a weekly plan table, a goal progress bar (demo), square bordered tiles for secondary content and a full-width lime CTA band.

**Best for:** programme / challenge sends, weekly training plans and audiences who respond to a motivational, performance tone.

**Note:** lime (`#c8f169`) is a new accent not present in the app; if chosen it should be agreed as a brand extension.

## Files

- `index.html` — standalone email HTML, English.
- `index.bg.html` — standalone email HTML, Bulgarian.
- `preview.html` — browser preview: EN + BG at desktop and mobile (375px) widths.

## Placeholders

All copy, numbers and the quote are **demo content**. Image areas are styled blocks. Links point to `#` and carry a `data-merge-tag` attribute (`{{unsubscribe_url}}`, `{{preferences_url}}`, `{{web_version_url}}`, `{{practice_url}}`, `{{program_url}}`, `{{article_url}}`, `{{app_url}}`); `{{first_name}}` is in the greeting. The postal address is a placeholder.

Design exploration only — not wired into the application.
