# Template 05 — Personal Letter

Reads like a personal email from a trainer rather than a designed newsletter (600px). Minimal chrome, a sender line with an initials avatar, Georgia letter body at 18px with one highlighted sentence, the featured practice as a dashed "try this with me" note, secondary content folded into a numbered "three small things" list, a reply prompt, a signature and a P.S.

**Best for:** building a relationship and getting replies; welcome series, founder/coach letters and low-volume community updates.

## Files

- `index.html` — standalone email HTML, English.
- `index.bg.html` — standalone email HTML, Bulgarian.
- `preview.html` — browser preview: EN + BG at desktop and mobile (375px) widths.

## Placeholders

All copy, numbers and the quote are **demo content**. Image areas are styled blocks. Links point to `#` and carry a `data-merge-tag` attribute (`{{unsubscribe_url}}`, `{{preferences_url}}`, `{{web_version_url}}`, `{{practice_url}}`, `{{program_url}}`, `{{article_url}}`, `{{app_url}}`); `{{first_name}}` is in the greeting. The postal address is a placeholder.

Design exploration only — not wired into the application.
